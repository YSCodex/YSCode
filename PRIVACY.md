# Privacy Policy
See the same policy on the website at `/privacy`. Summary: YSCode runs on your device and sends nothing to us by default.
Optional anonymous usage stats (off by default, random install ID, app/Android version, CPU type) and feedback you choose to send
are the only data we receive. Your prompts go directly to the AI provider you configure.

Sign-in with a ChatGPT plan (Codex) or Google Antigravity: the sign-in tokens stay on your device (Android Keystore / the app's private
storage) and go only to OpenAI or Google. We never receive them. First-launch setup downloads Linux packages (and, if you enable it, Google's
Antigravity CLI) directly from those servers to your phone. Last updated 2026-11-25.
